What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive catering solutions for every type of event across Kenya.

Wedding Catering

Make your special day memorable with elegant menus, beautiful presentation, and seamless service. From cocktail hour to the final toast, we handle every culinary detail.

Corporate Events

Impress clients and staff with professional catering for meetings, conferences, team-building events, and office parties. We offer flexible packages for any group size.

Private Parties

Birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, and family gatherings — we handle the food so you can enjoy the moment with your loved ones.

Outdoor Events

Garden parties, church functions, fundraisers, and community events — fully equipped for outdoor catering anywhere in Kenya with portable kitchens and serving stations.

Buffet Setup

Full buffet service with chafing dishes, serving staff, and a beautifully arranged spread that wows your guests. We handle setup, service, and cleanup.

Custom Menus

Have something specific in mind? We create tailored menus to match your event theme, dietary needs, and budget. Vegan, halal, gluten-free — we accommodate all.
