Our Story

From a Small Kitchen to Kenya's Favorite Caterer

Happy Plate Kitchen started with a simple belief: great food brings people together. What began as a passion for cooking has grown into one of Kenya's most trusted outside catering services.

We specialize in delivering delicious, fresh, and high-quality meals for every occasion. Our team of professional chefs and event coordinators work tirelessly to ensure your event is nothing short of spectacular.

From weddings and corporate functions to intimate family gatherings, we bring the same dedication, creativity, and flavor to every event we cater.
